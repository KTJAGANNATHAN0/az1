1. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • ArchZen employees
  • Contractors
  • Consultants
  • Service providers acting on behalf of ArchZen
  • AI-powered services delivered to clients

ArchZen is committed to the responsible use of AI based on the following principles:

  • Human oversight
  • Transparency
  • Security
  • Privacy
  • Accountability
  • Accuracy
  • Ethical use
  • Continuous improvement

AI supports our team—it does not replace professional judgement.

AI may be used to improve efficiency and service delivery in areas such as:

  • Technical documentation

  • Customer communication
  • AI-powered business solutions for clients

Human expertise remains central to every service we provide. Where AI is used:

  • Qualified professionals review outputs where appropriate.
  • Critical business decisions are never made solely by AI.
  • Security recommendations are validated before implementation.
  • Client deliverables are reviewed for quality and accuracy.

Protecting client information is one of our highest priorities. When using AI technologies, ArchZen aims to:

  • Minimise the amount of confidential information shared.

  • Apply appropriate security controls when selecting AI platforms.
  • Respect contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Handle information in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

AI tools used by ArchZen are selected with consideration for security and business suitability. Where appropriate, we consider factors including:

  • Vendor reputation
  • Security controls

  • Data handling processes
  • Access controls
  • Regulatory obligations
  • Business requirements

Not every publicly available AI platform is suitable for business use.

7. Responsible AI Use

ArchZen will not knowingly use AI to:

  • Generate malicious software.
  • Conduct cyber attacks.
  • Facilitate fraud or deception.
  • Create misleading or intentionally false information.
  • Infringe intellectual property rights.
  • Circumvent applicable laws or regulations.
  • Engage in discriminatory or unethical practices.

We expect our employees, contractors, and clients to use AI responsibly and lawfully.

8. AI Limitations

Although AI technologies continue to improve, they have limitations. AI-generated content may: contain factual inaccuracies; reflect outdated information; misunderstand context; produce incomplete results; or require technical verification.

AI should be considered an assistant—not an authoritative source.

9. Client AI Solutions

Where ArchZen designs, implements, or manages AI solutions for clients, we work collaboratively to ensure those solutions align with the client's objectives, operational requirements, and risk profile. Depending on the engagement, our AI services may include:
