Frequently asked questions

What services does ArchZen offer?

We provide a full suite of technology solutions tailored for growing organizations, including Managed IT Services, Cyber Security (Penetration Testing, EDR, Governance), and AI & Process Automation.

How does your AI and automation service work?

We begin with an AI Readiness Assessment to map your processes. From there, we design and implement custom LLM integrations, robotic process automation (RPA), and Microsoft 365 Copilot configurations to streamline your workflows.

Do you support businesses outside Melbourne?

While our primary certified engineering team is based in Melbourne, we support businesses and organizations remotely across all regions of Australia.

How do you ensure our data remains secure with AI integrations?

Security is at the core of all our designs. We deploy AI integrations using private cloud instances, ensure strict access control policies are enforced, and perform continuous compliance audits to protect your intellectual property.

What is your response time SLA for IT support?

We offer fast, SLA-backed response times depending on request priority. Critical issues are addressed immediately by our on-call Melbourne engineers, while general support tickets are picked up rapidly by our responsive helpdesk.

Can you work alongside our existing in-house IT team?

Absolutely. We provide team augmentation and co-managed IT models, assisting your in-house staff with specialized tasks like penetration testing, security monitoring, or AI implementation.

How do we get started?

You can book a no-obligation Discovery Call through our contact page. We will discuss your technology challenges, current setup, and map out a tailored strategy to make your IT systems secure and efficient.
